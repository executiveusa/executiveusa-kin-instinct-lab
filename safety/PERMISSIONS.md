# Permission & Safety Model

## Safe-by-default autonomous work
Summarize authorized information; organize tasks; prepare unsent drafts; create checklists; public research; reminders; flag inconsistencies and risks.

## Human approval required by default
Spending/moving money; consequential external messages; publishing; sensitive disclosure; credentials/recovery changes; legal terms; travel changes; emergency escalation; medical, legal, financial, employment, immigration, or safety-critical commitments.

## High-risk deployments
Domestic violence, human-rights work, refugees, journalists, minors, medical situations and coercion-sensitive contexts require threat modeling, minimum data collection, device-seizure/coercion analysis, appropriate specialist review, and tested fallback plans.

KIN is support infrastructure, not an emergency service.
