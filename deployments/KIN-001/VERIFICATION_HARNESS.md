## Three-task gate

Use a real messy workload. Produce stated outcome, facts vs assumptions, do now/delegate/schedule/ignore, one next action, approval points.

### Task 2 — Meeting/follow-up
Prepare one real meeting or follow-up sequence. Do not send externally without approval.

### Task 3 — Open-loop reduction
Organize real unfinished commitments into owner/date/next-action form.

Capture source context, output, owner confirmation and failures for each. Score PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL.

KIN-001 becomes VERIFIED only after all three tasks are real, evidence exists, no hidden consequential action occurred, the owner confirms material usefulness, and failures are recorded.

## Failure log
